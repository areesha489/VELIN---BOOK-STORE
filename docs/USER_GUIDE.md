# VELIN reader guide

VELIN is an online reading room. You can browse without an account. Buying, lists, reviews, and delivery tracking need a sign-in.

## Create an account

1. Choose **Sign in**, then **Create an account**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Phone is optional. You can add a shipping address later.

On the classroom computer, **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** signs you into a demo social profile stored on this machine. It does not contact Google or GitHub unless an editor has configured real keys.

## If you forget the password

1. Open **Forgot password** and enter the account email.
2. Use the link in the email. On the classroom computer the link is also shown on the next page, and a copy is written to the application log.
3. Choose a new password and sign in with it.

## Find a book

- Use the search box for a title, an author, a genre, or an ISBN.
- Use the genre and author menus to narrow the shelf.
- Sort by popularity, price, or release date.
- **Bestsellers** and **New arrivals** are shortcuts into the same catalog.

Each book page shows the cover, price, author, genre, ISBN, page count, and description. Prices are in rupees and include GST.

## Cart and wishlist

- **Add** puts one copy in the cart. On the book page you can choose a quantity.
- The cart shows the line prices, dispatch, and the total.
- Dispatch is ₹49. It is free when the book total reaches ₹999.
- Set a quantity to 0, or choose **Remove**, to drop a line.
- **Save** keeps a title on your wishlist for later. Open **List** in the header to see it.

You can fill a cart before you sign in. Checkout asks you to sign in, then keeps those books with your account.

## Checkout

1. Sign in and open the cart.
2. Choose a saved address or enter a new one.
3. Choose a saved payment method or add a card or UPI id.
4. Place the order.

For a card, use `4242 4242 4242 4242` with any future expiry and the name on the card. VELIN checks the number, then keeps only the card brand and the last four digits. Nothing is sent to a bank. This shop is for training.

## After the order

Open **Orders** on your account. Each order has a number and a timeline:

Placed, Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered.

When the parcel leaves the desk, the editor adds a carrier and a tracking code. That code is shown on the order. Use it with the carrier named beside it. If an order is cancelled, the copies return to the shelf.

## Reviews

On a book page, signed-in readers can leave one note and a rating from 1 to 5. You can update that note later or delete it. You can like someone else’s note. You cannot like your own.

## Profile

From your name in the header you can change your name, email, and phone, replace your password, manage addresses, and manage payment methods. One address and one payment method can be the default.

## If something goes wrong

- “Those credentials do not match” means the email or password is wrong. Five failures in a minute pauses sign-in for that email on that network.
- “Not enough copies” means another reader took the last books. Lower the quantity.
- “This page expired” means the form was open too long. Go back, refresh, and submit again.
- “This account has been paused” means an editor turned the account off.

## Editor desk

Editors sign in and open **Desk**. From there they add and edit books, genres, and authors, update order status and tracking, and pause or promote reader accounts. A reader cannot open the desk.
